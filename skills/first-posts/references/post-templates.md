## Hashtag strategy

Large (1M+): #saas #startup #founder #entrepreneur #indiehacker
Medium (100K-1M): #buildinpublic #solopreneur #productlaunch #[niche]
Niche (<100K): #[product category] #[specific audience] #[pain point]

Mix all three. Max 15 on Instagram. Max 3 on LinkedIn.
