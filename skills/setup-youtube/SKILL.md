# YouTube channel setup

## Why YouTube for SaaS solopreneurs

YouTube is the second-largest search engine. Tutorial videos and product demos rank in Google search results and drive high-intent traffic. A single "how to [solve problem]" video can generate leads for years.

---

## What to collect

- Product name and one-liner
- Target audience
- Founder name (YouTube works best as personal + brand)
- Google account available?
- Camera/setup: phone, dedicated camera, or screen recording only?

## Step 3 â First video strategy

### Video 1 â Product demo (Week 1)
**Format**: Screen recording + voiceover (NO camera needed)
**Title**: "How to [outcome] with [Product] in [X minutes]"
**Length**: 5-10 minutes

### Video 2 â Problem-solution (Week 2)
**Title**: "Why [common approach] doesn't work (and what to do instead)"
**Length**: 7-12 minutes
**Goal**: Rank for the problem your audience searches for

### Video 3 â Build in public (Week 3)
**Title**: "I built [Product] in [X months] â here's what happened"
**Length**: 8-15 minutes
**Goal**: Build connection, attract early adopters

## Step 5 â Posting schedule

- Minimum: 1 video/month (results matter more than frequency)
- Recommended: 2 videos/month (1 tutorial + 1 build-in-public)
- First 10 videos: prioritize searchable tutorials over entertaining content

---

## Step 6 â Connect YouTube to the rest of the stack

- Add YouTube link to website footer

- Repurpose video scripts as blog posts
- Add YouTube link to email signature and onboarding sequences
- Add YouTube to Organization schema (sameAs array)
