# 3-month content plan

## Collect if not already known
- Product name and target audience
- Current stage (pre-launch, just launched, growing)
- Realistic time per week for content (30 min / 1 hour / 2+ hours)

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Focus | Share |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Education | Teach something useful to your audience | 40% |
| Product | Features, updates, behind-the-scenes | 20% |
| Story | Founder journey, lessons, mistakes | 25% |
| Social proof | Results, testimonials, milestones | 15% |

Adjust by stage:
- Pre-launch: Story 40% + Education 40%
- Post-launch growing: Social proof 25% + Product 25%

## Monthly themes

Month 1 — Awareness: Who are you? What problem do you solve?
Blog: "The [problem] guide for [audience]"
Social: Problem awareness + founder story

Month 2 — Authority: Become the expert. Teach, share insights, show results.
Blog: How-to content targeting main keywords
Social: Education posts + early user results

Month 3 — Conversion: Turn followers into users. Clear CTAs, proof, offers.
Blog: Comparison and alternative content
Social: Social proof + feature spotlights + upgrade nudge

## Weekly cadence by time budget

30 min/week: 2 Twitter/X, 1 LinkedIn, 1 Threads
1 hour/week: 3 Twitter/X (1 thread), 2 LinkedIn, 3 Threads, 1 Instagram
2+ hours/week: Daily Twitter/X, 3 LinkedIn, Daily Threads, 3 Instagram, 1 blog post

## Output format

Generate week-by-week for all 12 weeks:

MONTH 1 — AWARENESS
Week 1 — Theme: [specific]
Instagram: [idea + angle]
Twitter/X: [thread idea]
LinkedIn: [post idea]
Threads: [hot take or question]
Blog: [title + target keyword]
